Engineering Showcase 01

Autonomous GPS Waypoint Quadcopter & GIS Ground Station

A full-stack embedded aerospace engineering project: converting OpenStreetMap vector graphs into downsampled C++ PROGMEM headers and executing an 8-state deterministic FSM flight controller within 682 bytes of SRAM.

Three-Stage Decoupled Avionics Architecture

Stage 1 (High-Level Python Host): Queries OpenStreetMap vector networks via OSMNx and NetworkX to compute geographic shortest paths across real Ethiopian urban corridors. Trajectories are downsampled via Douglas-Peucker spatial compression to guarantee MCU SRAM safety.

Stage 2 (Header Compilation): Exports coordinates into fixed-width C++ struct arrays with PROGMEM macros, allowing the 8-bit MCU to access full paths without dynamic memory allocation or heap fragmentation risks.

Stage 3 (Closed-Loop AVR Firmware): Onboard ATmega328P runs an interrupt-safe 700ms control cadence, parsing NMEA strings from NEO-6M GPS, angular kinematics from MPU-6050, and proximity alerts from forward IR sensors.

Deterministic Non-Blocking Control Loop

Rather than using blocking delays (delay()) that blind the microcontroller to incoming telemetry, the firmware operates on a precise 700ms millis() scheduler driving a deterministic 8-state Finite State Machine:

  • STATE_IDLE: Motors disengaged; awaits UART commands (START, STOP, LIST, RTH).
  • STATE_NAVIGATING: Calculates Great Circle bearing and Haversine distance to active waypoint; adjusts differential motor thrust.
  • STATE_AVOIDING_OBSTACLE: Forward IR detection triggers immediate starboard evasive maneuver until line-of-sight clears.
  • STATE_WAITING: 5.0m arrival radius triggers 5,000ms loiter/payload delivery hold.
  • STATE_RETURNING (RTH): Automatically reverses waypoint array stack to navigate drone back to launch origin.
  • STATE_EMERGENCY_HALT: Hardware interrupt immediately cuts PWM to all 4 motors if ball tilt sensor detects airframe inversion (>45° tilt).

Hardware Architecture & Power Regulation

Dual-Rail Power Topology: Quadcopter high-current coreless DC lift motors produce significant inductive back-EMF that resets sensitive microcontrollers. The system implements isolated dual rails:

  • High-Current Rail (9V): Powers four DC motors switched via 2N1711 NPN BJT transistors with 100Ω base-protection resistors and flyback suppression diodes.
  • Regulated Logic Rail (5V): Powers ATmega328P MCU, U-Blox NEO-6M GPS receiver (SoftwareSerial on D2/D3), and InvenSense MPU-6050 (Hardware I2C on A4/A5).
  • Safety Interlocks: Omnidirectional ball tilt switch on D12 with internal pull-up resistor. Active-LOW interrupt disengages motor bases instantly upon frame inversion.

Research Showcase 02

Quantum Cryptanalysis: Shor's Algorithm vs. RSA

Published theoretical review analyzing quantum superposition, Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT) period finding and the impending obsolescence of classical integer factorization cryptosystems.

SSRN Elsevier Preprint Published Nov 2024 SSRN ID: 4992090

The Cruciality of Shor's Algorithm in Breaking RSA Encryption

Author: Bilisuma Jabesa Merga (Independent High School Scholar)

Abstract: Public-key cryptography, particularly RSA, relies on the classical intractability of factoring large composite semiprimes \(N = p \cdot q\). For classical architectures, algorithms like the General Number Field Sieve (GNFS) require sub-exponential time. Shor’s algorithm fundamentally circumvents this barrier by reducing factorization to order finding. By exploiting quantum superposition and constructive phase interference via the Quantum Fourier Transform (QFT), Shor’s algorithm evaluates modular exponentiation periods in polynomial time \(\mathcal{O}((\log N)^3)\), proving that fault-tolerant quantum computers will completely compromise RSA private keys.
